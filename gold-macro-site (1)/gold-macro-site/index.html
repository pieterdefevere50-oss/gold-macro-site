Methodology, sources & limits
  • Data-driven factors come from FRED each run: 10Y TIPS yield, Fed broad dollar index (not ICE DXY), 2Y yield vs fed funds.
  • Thresholds: real yield ±10bp and dollar ±1% over 4 weeks; Fed path = 2Y vs fed funds ±25bp.
  • Weights (1–5): real yields 5 · Fed path 5 · dollar 4 · central banks 4 · fiscal 3 · geopolitics 3 · news 2 (3 if ≥3 outlets).
  • Hand-written factors show a review date. ETF flows and futures positioning are not covered.
  • News is Google News RSS, classified by direction-aware rules with duplicates merged. It can still mis-tag; check the source.
  • Each update logs price and score, so you can judge whether the bias leads price. It summarises drivers; it is not a forecast.
